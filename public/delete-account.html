Delete your Zaylo account

Last updated 11 November 2026

You can delete your Zaylo account and the data in it yourself, at any time, from inside the app or on the web. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Zaylo on your phone, or sign in at zaylo.cc.
  2. Open your account menu and choose Settings.
  3. Open Security & Privacy and choose Delete account.
  4. Type the confirmation the screen asks for, then confirm.

If you want a copy of your records first, use Export Data in Settings before you delete.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in account.
  • Your expenses, income, bills, savings goals, Smart Cart lists and AutoTrack records.
  • Workspaces you own, including what other members added to them. Members are removed from those workspaces.
  • Your messages and files in workspace chat, your support requests and their attachments, and your notification settings.
  • Your AI permission record and any AI feedback you sent.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Records you added to a workspace owned by someone else stay in that workspace, without your name.
  • Copies of your backups that you saved to your own cloud storage or device are yours; Zaylo cannot delete them.
  • Database backups made by Zaylo's hosting provider may contain your data until they expire, on the provider's normal backup schedule. They are not used for anything else.

Can't sign in?

If you cannot get into your account, email hearteagle28@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We will ask you to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can delete individual records and whole workspaces inside the app, and remove the copy on a device with Remove data from this device in Settings.
