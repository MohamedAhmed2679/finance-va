Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Zaylo helps you record spending, income, bills and savings goals, and share a workspace with people you choose. This page explains what we keep and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address or phone number, and the sign-in method you use (email, Microsoft, Google or text message).
  • What you enter: expenses, income, bills, savings goals, workspace names, chat messages and support messages.
  • Basic settings such as currency, language and theme.

When you sign in with Microsoft or Google we receive only your name, email address and an account identifier. We do not receive your password or access to your mailbox, files or contacts.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your records, sync them across your devices, share a workspace with members you add, send you notifications you ask for, and answer support messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the app is served by Vercel.

AI features

AI features are optional and are off until you allow them. When you use one, Zaylo sends the data that feature needs to Google (Gemini API), which produces the result. Depending on the feature this is: your question and a summary of the current workspace (totals by category and month, your budget, your 15 most recent expenses with date, shop, category and amount, your bills and your savings goals); a voice note, receipt photo, PDF or text you submit; a photo of a price tag; or, if you turn on AutoTrack AI reading, a bank alert with account numbers masked. Your name, email address, phone number, passwords and PINs are not sent. The app shows this list and asks for your permission before the first use, records when you allow or withdraw it, and lets you withdraw at any time in Settings under AI and data.

Who can see it

You, the members of workspaces you share, and Zaylo administrators when needed to provide support or keep the service safe. Administrator actions are logged.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your records at any time, export them from Settings, and delete your account and the data that belongs only to you yourself in Settings, under Security & Privacy. You can also turn optional features such as AI and notifications off again whenever you like.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data yourself in Settings. The steps, what is deleted and what is kept are on the account deletion page.
